Banana Nut Bread

Ingredients

  • 8 large bananas
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 c. butter
  • 2 c. sugar
  • 4 c. flour
  • 2 tsp. soda
  • 1 tsp. salt

Directions

  1. Beat the bananas and eggs together.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar, then add the banana and egg mixture.
  3. Mix in the flour, soda and salt.
  4. Bake at 325° for 50 minutes.

Bonnie's Hot Rolls

Ingredients

  • 3 c. milk, scalded
  • 1 cube (stick) margarine, plus more melted for the tops
  • 5 tbs. sugar
  • 1 tbs. salt
  • 8 c. flour
  • 1 c. water
  • 2 packages yeast (a note says 3)

Directions

  1. Mix the dough, knead and let rise.
  2. Roll ½ to ¼ inch thick and cut in squares.
  3. Put 3 or 4 squares in each cup of a greased muffin tin. Brush melted margarine on each.
  4. Let rise at least ½ hour.
  5. Bake at 400° for 10–12 minutes.

Basic Quiche

Serves 8 as an appetizer or 6 for lunch.

Ingredients

  • 1 (9-inch) pie crust, rubbed with butter and placed in the freezer
  • 12 slices bacon (the cook notes "I use 7")
  • 4 eggs
  • ¼ c. chopped green onions
  • 2 c. whipping cream
  • ¼ lb. Swiss cheese, grated (1½ cups)
  • ¾ tsp. salt
  • 1 T. flour
  • ⅛ tsp. pepper
  • ⅛ tsp. nutmeg
  • ⅛ tsp. sugar

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp and chop into ½-inch pieces.
  3. Beat the eggs, cream, salt, pepper, nutmeg, sugar and flour until blended.
  4. Mix the onions, cheese and bacon together and place in the bottom of the frozen shell. Pour the cream mixture over the top.
  5. Bake 15 minutes at 425°, then lower the heat to 300° for 45 minutes.

Focaccia

From Gigliola Nordman

Ingredients

  • 3¾ c. flour
  • 1 small potato, boiled with salt, peeled and mashed
  • 2 cubes (cakes) of yeast
  • 1 c. lukewarm water
  • Olive oil
  • Salt

Directions

  1. Combine the flour, potato, yeast and water.
  2. Knead 3–5 minutes. Cover and let rise 1½–2 hours.
  3. Oil a pan and spread the dough in it. Poke holes in the dough with your finger.
  4. Sprinkle with salt and olive oil. Bake 30 minutes at 375°.

Chile Verde

From Claudia Mackin (newspaper clipping)

Ingredients

  • 1 pound pork, cubed (or beef)
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced (or garlic salt)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 8 to 10 green chilies
  • 2 to 3 jalapeño chilies (optional)
  • 2 cups water
  • Flour tortillas, for serving

Directions

Clam Chowder (with red wine vinegar)

Serves 8. A note on the card says "double recipe."

Ingredients

  • 1 c. onions
  • 1 c. celery
  • 2 c. diced potatoes
  • 2 cans minced clams
  • ¾ c. butter
  • ¾ c. flour
  • 1½ tsp. salt
  • Pinch of pepper
  • 1 qt. half and half
  • 2 T. red wine vinegar
  • Sprinkle of cayenne

Directions

  1. Cover the vegetables with water and cook. Do not drain.
  2. Drain the clam juice into the vegetables.
  3. Melt the butter and add the flour, salt and pepper. Add the half and half and blend thoroughly with a wire whisk.
  4. Add the undrained vegetables and the vinegar. Add the clams.
  5. Simmer slowly until it thickens.

Linda's Ham & Cheese Casserole

Make it the night before.

Ingredients

  • 16 slices bread, crusts cut off
  • Butter
  • About ¾ lb. mild cheddar and diced ham, total
  • Seasoned salt or other seasonings
  • Mayonnaise
  • 8 eggs
  • ¾ qt. milk

Gravy

  • Cream of mushroom soup, Franco-American brown gravy and 1 small can mushrooms, or any gravy you like

Directions

  1. Butter the bottom of a 9×15 pan. Layer 8 slices of bread in the pan.
  2. Butter the top of the bread. Sprinkle with the grated cheese and diced ham, then the seasonings.
  3. Put another layer of 8 slices of bread over the ham and cheese. Butter the top and spread lightly with mayonnaise.
  4. Beat the eggs and milk in a blender for ½ minute. Pour over the bread.
  5. Cover with foil and refrigerate overnight.
  6. Bake uncovered the next day at 300° for 1 hour 15 minutes. Serve with the gravy.

Sausage Appetizers

Makes about 4 dozen.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. pork sausage
  • ½ t. sweet basil leaves
  • ⅓ c. minced water chestnuts
  • Paprika

Directions

  1. Combine the first 3 ingredients and mix with your hands until well blended.
  2. Use a heaping teaspoonful for each meatball. Dip your hands in cold water and shape into smooth balls.

Caramel Popcorn

Ingredients

  • ¼ or ½ c. margarine
  • 1 can Eagle Brand milk
  • 1 cup light Karo syrup
  • Pinch of salt (¼ tsp.)
  • 2¼ c. brown sugar (1 pkg.)
  • 8 qts. (30 cups) popped popcorn

Directions

  1. Bring the Karo syrup, salt and brown sugar to a boil. Add the milk and margarine.
  2. Bring to the soft-ball stage, 238°.
  3. Pour over the popcorn and stir to coat. Makes about 30 balls.

Can also be used for candied apples.

Directions

Double Decker Fudge

From the kitchen of Brenda. Makes 4 lbs.

Ingredients

  • ¼ c. butter, melted
  • ½ c. Hershey's cocoa
  • 1 t. vanilla
  • 2 c. (12 oz.) Reese's peanut butter chips, divided
  • 4½ c. sugar
  • 1 jar (7 oz.) marshmallow creme
  • 1½ c. evaporated milk
  • ¼ c. butter

Directions

  1. Line a 13×9 pan with foil. Put 1 c. peanut butter chips in a medium bowl and set aside. In a second bowl, blend the melted butter, cocoa and vanilla until smooth, then add the other 1 c. chips.
  2. In a heavy 4-quart saucepan, combine the sugar, marshmallow creme, evaporated milk and ¼ c. butter. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until it comes to a rolling boil. Boil and stir 5 minutes.
  3. Remove from the heat. Immediately add half the hot mixture to the bowl of plain peanut butter chips. Pour the rest into the cocoa mixture and stir to blend.
  4. Beat the peanut butter mixture until the chips are completely melted. Spread evenly in the pan.
  5. Beat the cocoa mixture until the chips melt and it thickens. Spread evenly over the peanut butter layer.
  6. Cool, lift out of the pan, remove the foil and cut into squares. Store airtight in a cool, dry place.
